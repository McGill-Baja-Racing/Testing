# Subsystem Lead quick start

**You own the engineering question, acceptance criteria and conclusions.** Other members suggest tests through you; Managers' vehicle-level concerns also become requests through the appropriate Subsystem Lead.

1. Open [Test request](https://github.com/McGill-Baja-Racing/Testing/issues/new?template=test-request.yml) after rollout; [local fields](../../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/test-request.yml). Describe the physical question, measurements, outputs, intended decision, sufficient evidence, consequences and preferred/latest useful timing. If timing is unknown, identify the decision milestone. Link relevant requirements. Confirm completeness in the issue.
2. Answer specialist clarification before Managers prioritize. You need not choose sensors or sampling rates. Define operating context with Electronics; keep the technical objective under your ownership. The Testing Manager can return unclear plans, but cannot change your objective unilaterally.
3. For verification/comparisons, define applicable acceptance criteria and engineering basis before interpreting evidence. Exploratory/diagnostic work needs a definite question and sufficient evidence, not an invented pass/fail threshold. [Technical standard](../standards/technical-testing.md).
4. Agree a Test Lead, Instrumentation Owner, Analysis Owner and analysis due date during planning. Coordinate with the Testing Manager; do not assume Managers' value approval commits resource teams.
5. At the test, jointly decide go/no-go with the Testing Manager, subject to required technical/safety clearance. Approve the engineering interpretation after analysis; [closure](closure.md) explains the record check.

## Own verification over time

Create a focused [DVP&R](../../templates/dvp-r.md): important requirements, safety/performance goals, assumptions and known failure risks. Begin with a useful subset, not an exhaustive catalogue. [Example](../../verification/subsystems/drivetrain.md).

Update evidence/applicability and status after results, significant design changes or failures. A closed attempt does not verify a requirement. Invalid/inconclusive evidence needs a linked follow-up for unresolved objectives and leaves the requirement unverified. Bring gaps to Managers at major design milestones. [Verification standard](../standards/verification-strategy.md).
