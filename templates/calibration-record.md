# Calibration or reference-verification record

Required for measurement equipment where calibration/reference verification is applicable. If inapplicable, record the reason and technical owner's judgment. No universal expiry period or calibration recipe applies.

- **Identity:** stable record ID/revision, physical device ID, installation/configuration scope, author/operator/date; supersedes.
- **Method:** procedure/version; quantities/range/points; conditions; reference equipment/certificate IDs, traceability chain and reference validity.
- **Evidence:** original readings/data links; coefficients/scaling and units; residuals/check results; uncertainty/basis and limitations.
- **Validity:** date interval OR usage/condition review triggers with rationale; applicable environment/range; invalidation triggers.
- **Decision:** acceptable for specified use / blocked, with intended-use limits and technical owner identity/date/comment.

Optional: plots, uncertainty budget and processing code. Link this record from the [equipment/install record](sensor-record.md) and test; retain superseded versions. A valid standalone calibration does not prove the installed system ready.
