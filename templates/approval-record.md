## Screening record

- Category: ready / existing needs adaptation / new development / unknown feasibility / no instrumentation support.
- Preparation effort: low / medium / high; main tasks; estimate/range and rough lead time.
- Dependencies/uncertainty; physical needs requiring clarification; screen author/date.
- No instrumentation support means explicitly justified no DAQ need or unavailable support; state which. It never waives required evidence.
- Technical complexity: low / medium / high, with reason. Operational safety risk: owner assessment and controls, separately.

## Capacity and investigation details

Per team: manager acknowledgment; WIP limit/current commitments; allocated effort/time window; named work owner and due date; scope; dependencies; linked preparation issues; review date. Partial or declined commitment keeps uncommitted work out of preparation. Investigation consumes capacity too. At its cap stop and review: proceed to separately authorized full preparation, revise/screen again, defer or decline.

## Configuration/scope change

Record old/new revisions, affected approvals, Testing Manager reassessment scope with technical/safety owners, evidence to repeat, renewed sign-offs and schedule impact. Substantial scope changes return to screening and reauthorization. Proposed pilot convention: leadership representative/delegate records full-preparation authority separately; approve this delegation during rollout.
